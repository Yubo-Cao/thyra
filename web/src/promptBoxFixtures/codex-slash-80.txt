

• The terminal wakes in a coat of black,
  A small white moon at the end of a track.
  I type my name, and the silence replies
  With paths to the rooms where yesterday lies.
  The cursor keeps time with a patient blink,
  Allowing the space that I need to think.
  A command slips out like a key in a door;
  The screen fills with things I was looking for.
  Some lines turn red, and I sit up straight,
  Then find one small letter and alter its fate.
  Outside, the buses go hissing through rain;
  Inside, I call an old process again.
  The files have names I no longer recall,
  But their dates mark a summer, a move, and a fall.
